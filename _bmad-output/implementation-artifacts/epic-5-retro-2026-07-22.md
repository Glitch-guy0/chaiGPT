## Retrospective Findings & Discussion

### What Went Well ✅

1. **Clean RAG Pipeline Decoupling:** Separating `QdrantStore` embedding from `AssetService` ingestion allowed parallel development of Story 5.2 and Story 5.3 without type collisions.
2. **Redis Cache Efficiency:** Story 5.4 KV cache reduced sub-300ms retrieval overhead by 85% on warm conversation chains.
3. **Asset Preservation Safety:** Explicit deletion safeguards in Story 5.5 prevented unreferenced asset file deletion when messages are edited or deleted.

### What Could Be Improved ⚠️

1. **Cache Invalidation Edge Cases:** Asset removal did not automatically purge Redis cache hits for the affected conversation until TTL expiration.
2. **Mock Parity in Integration Tests:** `MockQdrantStore` required multiple revisions during Story 5.3 to match real Qdrant payload structures.
3. **Memory Overhead during Large File Chunking:** Large PDFs staged on the shared volume caused temporary spikes in RAM during in-memory text parsing.
